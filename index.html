FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to pay before the consultation?
No. There is no payment required simply to submit your project details or have the initial consultation.
Can I work on just one room?
Yes. You can tell us about one room or multiple areas of your home.
What if I already know exactly what I want?
That's fine. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll consider it alongside the space, budget, and other requirements.
What if I don't know exactly what I want?
That's fine too. Tell us what you don't like about the current space and what you'd like it to feel like. We can help work out the direction.
Can you work with furniture I already have?
Yes. Existing items can be considered as part of the project.
Do all items have to be brand new?
Not necessarily. Your preferences around new, reused, or secondhand items can be considered when planning the project.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the space, what you want to change, the items/materials involved, and your budget. We'll discuss the project with you before agreeing on the scope and cost.
Do you handle the actual work?
Nest Home Improvements focuses on delivering the project — including planning, sourcing, and coordinating the work with the right artisans and partners.
What happens after I submit my project?
A Nest consultant reviews the information you've provided and reaches out to discuss the project and next steps.
